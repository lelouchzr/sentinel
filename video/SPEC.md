# SENTINEL film specification

GOAL: Show a stranger how scattered events become an inspectable, persistent investigation.

OUTPUT: Hyperframes; 1920×1080; 30 fps; H.264 MP4 with AAC; English text; no voiceover.
TIME: 115.2 seconds × 30 = 3,456 frames. Music: 150 BPM, 12 frames per beat, 48 per bar.

LOOK: Actual Sentinel terminal fills the proof shots. Ground #0a1119; ink #e8f2ee;
mint #79e4cb for evidence and continuity; amber #e7bc74 for uncertainty. A mint ground
#d9eee4 carries the ingestion data beat; amber #efd49c carries the benign-context beat.
Manrope 650–800 at 64–132 px for titles; 30–40 px for explanations. JetBrains Mono
for source references and the authentic terminal rasterization. Margins 96 px; no fake OS chrome.

MOTION: Main entrances 12–18 frames, power3.out; camera 18–24 frames, power2.inOut;
exits 8–12 frames, power2.in. Cut, mask wipe, evidence-line expansion and focused punch-in
have distinct jobs. Titles stop after landing. Proof is held long enough to read.

small floating screenshots; a logo alone at frame zero; repeating slide-up entrances;
unreadable provenance pasted as a decorative block; a simulated live API response.

QA: Inspect every scene midpoint, the alert-to-case transition, the live-activity capture,
the session/event source, the backup approval context, and the final export/reopen.
Inspect cut strips and phone-size contact sheets. Validate duration, frame count, dimensions,
