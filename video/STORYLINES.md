# Storylines considered

## Selected: Follow the session

One fictional account travels from scattered records to an inspectable case. The film follows
the recorded session through authentication, access, download, source inspection and review.
Typography cuts on the music; actual terminal captures provide the proof. The first two seconds
show a real failed-login record alongside the question "What happened?". The payoff is a source
reference and a saved, reopened investigation rather than a verdict of compromise.

Look: Sentinel's dark terminal, large Manrope titles, restrained mint and amber, with a light
mint data scene and amber authorization scene for contrast. Sound: edited "Cipher" by Kevin
MacLeod at its documented 150 BPM, with a small number of licensed CC0 interface effects.

## Alternative: The report first

Begin with the exported report, zoom into an event ID, then reverse the journey into the source
records and replay how the case was built. The form is an object-to-object zoom chain; the look
is a bright report surface giving way to the dark terminal. Music opens when the original event
appears. This gives the deliverable more attention but delays the product workflow.

## Alternative: A volume alert is not a verdict

Open on the two accounts with the same recorded byte total, then reveal the different session
and job context. A split view converges into one case review. The look alternates amber and mint;
the music pauses at the benign explanation. This is a strong evidence lesson but gives ingestion,
MCP and persistence less screen time than the selected storyline.

The owner requested autonomous production and the final video; no selection stop was required.
